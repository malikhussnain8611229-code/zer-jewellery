Our Heritage

Born from the
Language of Gold

ZÉR — the Persian word for gold — was born from a simple but profound belief: that jewellery is not merely decoration. It is memory, identity, and grace made tangible. Every piece carries within it a story — of the woman who will wear it, the hands that crafted it, and the generations of artisans who perfected the art.

Founded in the heart of Pakistan's jewellery tradition, ZÉR bridges the gap between ancestral craftsmanship and contemporary luxury. We work with master karigars whose families have practiced their craft for centuries, honouring their knowledge while designing for the modern woman.

Our collections are born at the intersection of quiet luxury and editorial boldness — jewellery that speaks before you do.

Handcrafted Always

Every ZÉR piece is made by hand. We believe that the mark of human craft — its warmth, its slight imperfections, its soul — is irreplaceable by any machine.

Honest Materials

We use only ethically sourced metals and gemstones. Every gold hallmark, every certified diamond, every cultured pearl is traceable and responsibly obtained.

Made for Real Women

ZÉR jewellery is designed for the woman who lives fully — not just for special occasions, but for Tuesday mornings and quiet dinners and moments that are hers alone.
